About the Model

The system uses Logistic Regression to perform binary classification. The model estimates the probability of depression risk based on the information entered by the student.

The estimated probability is then used to place the result into one of three prototype screening categories: Low Risk, Moderate Risk, or High Risk.

Machine Learning Workflow

1

Dataset

Student Depression Dataset containing academic, lifestyle and mental-health related attributes.

2

Preprocessing

Data cleaning, categorical encoding, numerical conversion and feature scaling.

3

Training

Logistic Regression is trained using an 80/20 stratified train-test split.

4

Prediction

The model estimates the probability of depression risk for new student input.
